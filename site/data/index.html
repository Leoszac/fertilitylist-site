Open Data: US Fertility Clinic Dataset

Last updated 2026-11-13

We publish the processed dataset behind FertilityList so researchers, journalists and patients can check and reuse it.

Download

  • us-clinics.csv: 452 US clinics, CDC reporting year 2022. Includes live births per intended egg retrieval by age, total cycles, services, SART membership, lab accreditation and the FertilityList Score.

License and attribution

The dataset is licensed under CC BY 4.0. Please credit: FertilityList (fertilitylist.com), based on CDC National ART Surveillance System data. The underlying CDC data is in the public domain.

Notes

  • Rates are percentages as published by the CDC. Empty cells mean the CDC suppressed the value, showed it as a small-denominator fraction, or did not report it.
  • The scoring method is described on the methodology page.
  • Data retrieved 2026-11-13. It is refreshed monthly when the CDC publishes updates.
