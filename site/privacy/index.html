Privacy Policy

Last updated November 13, 2026

FertilityList is designed to collect as little information about you as possible.

What we collect

  • No accounts, no forms. You can use the whole site without giving us any personal information.
  • Traffic statistics (Google Analytics 4). We use Google Analytics to understand, in aggregate, which pages are read and how visitors arrive (for example from a search engine). Google Analytics may set first-party cookies and receives information such as the pages you view, approximate location derived from your IP address, and device and browser type. We have turned off Google signals, ad personalization and advertising storage, and we do not send Google any information that identifies you. For visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, analytics cookies are disabled by default and only cookieless, aggregate measurements are sent. Google processes this data under its Privacy Policy; see also how Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider keeps standard web server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) for security and operations, for a limited period.

Third-party services and links

Clinic pages link to Google Maps so you can see a clinic's location and read its Google reviews. When you follow such a link, you leave FertilityList, and Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service apply. If we ever embed Google Maps Platform features directly on our pages, use of those features is subject to the same Google policies and the Google Maps/Google Earth Additional Terms of Service.

Health information

We do not ask for, collect or store any health information about visitors.

Your choices

You can opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on, by blocking cookies in your browser, or with any standard content blocker. The site works the same either way. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Changes

If we change this policy, for example when introducing an enquiry form, we will update this page before the change takes effect.

Medical disclaimer: FertilityList provides general information and publicly reported data for educational purposes only. It is not medical advice and does not replace a consultation with a qualified healthcare professional. Clinic success rates depend on patient characteristics and are not a prediction of any individual outcome. Read the full disclaimer.
