Key facts

  • In 2022, Reproductive Medicine Associates of Philadelphia reported 2,143 ART cycles to the CDC.
  • For patients under 35 using their own eggs, 62.8% of intended egg retrievals resulted in a live birth, compared with a national figure of 49.7%.
  • FertilityList Score: 4.9 out of 5, ranked #1 of 11 reporting clinics in Pennsylvania.

Data source: CDC National ART Surveillance System, reporting year 2022, retrieved 2026-11-13.
