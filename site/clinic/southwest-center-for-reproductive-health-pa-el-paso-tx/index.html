How the FertilityList Score is built

This clinic has no score because it reported fewer than 20 intended egg retrievals with comparable rates. Small samples can swing widely from year to year, so we do not rank them. Learn more.

The CDC cautions that comparing clinics may not be meaningful, because clinics treat different patient populations and use different methods. Use these figures as a starting point for questions, not as a prediction of your own chances.
