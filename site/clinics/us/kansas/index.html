Kansas IVF clinics: common questions

How many IVF clinics are in Kansas?

5 clinics in Kansas reported IVF outcomes to the CDC for 2022, performing 3,224 ART cycles in total.

Which fertility clinic in Kansas performs the most IVF cycles?

Center for Advanced Reproductive Medicine reported the most ART cycles in Kansas for 2022: 1,270. Cycle volume reflects experience but is not by itself a measure of success.

What is the IVF success rate in Kansas?

For patients under 35 using their own eggs, the median across 5 reporting clinics in Kansas was 50.8% live births per intended egg retrieval in 2022, compared with 49.7% nationally (CDC). Rates fall with age and vary by clinic.

Which clinics in Kansas offer egg freezing, donor eggs or gestational carriers?

According to the services clinics reported to the CDC for 2022, 5 of 5 offer egg freezing, 3 offer donor eggs and 3 work with gestational carriers. Use the service filters in the clinic list on this page to see which ones.

What is the best-ranked IVF clinic in Kansas?

By the FertilityList Score, which weighs CDC-reported live-birth rates against national figures by age, cycle volume, reporting consistency, SART membership and lab accreditation, the highest-ranked clinic in Kansas is Center for Advanced Reproductive Medicine (3.9 out of 5). The CDC cautions that comparing clinics may not be meaningful because clinics treat different patient populations.
