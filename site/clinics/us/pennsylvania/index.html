Pennsylvania IVF clinics: common questions

How many IVF clinics are in Pennsylvania?

11 clinics in Pennsylvania reported IVF outcomes to the CDC for 2022, performing 11,936 ART cycles in total. Another 2 clinics appear in CDC records without 2022 outcome data.

Which fertility clinic in Pennsylvania performs the most IVF cycles?

Shady Grove Fertility-Pennsylvania reported the most ART cycles in Pennsylvania for 2022: 3,084. Cycle volume reflects experience but is not by itself a measure of success.

What is the IVF success rate in Pennsylvania?

For patients under 35 using their own eggs, the median across 10 reporting clinics in Pennsylvania was 52.5% live births per intended egg retrieval in 2022, compared with 49.7% nationally (CDC). Rates fall with age and vary by clinic.

Which clinics in Pennsylvania offer egg freezing, donor eggs or gestational carriers?

According to the services clinics reported to the CDC for 2022, 11 of 11 offer egg freezing, 9 offer donor eggs and 10 work with gestational carriers. Use the service filters in the clinic list on this page to see which ones.

What is the best-ranked IVF clinic in Pennsylvania?

By the FertilityList Score, which weighs CDC-reported live-birth rates against national figures by age, cycle volume, reporting consistency, SART membership and lab accreditation, the highest-ranked clinic in Pennsylvania is Reproductive Medicine Associates of Philadelphia (4.9 out of 5). The CDC cautions that comparing clinics may not be meaningful because clinics treat different patient populations.
