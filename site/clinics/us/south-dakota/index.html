South Dakota IVF clinics: common questions

How many IVF clinics are in South Dakota?

1 clinic in South Dakota reported IVF outcomes to the CDC for 2022, performing 640 ART cycles in total.

What is the IVF success rate in South Dakota?

For patients under 35 using their own eggs, the median across 1 reporting clinic in South Dakota was 38.5% live births per intended egg retrieval in 2022, compared with 49.7% nationally (CDC). Rates fall with age and vary by clinic.

Which clinics in South Dakota offer egg freezing, donor eggs or gestational carriers?

According to the services clinics reported to the CDC for 2022, 1 of 1 offer egg freezing, 1 offer donor eggs and 1 work with gestational carriers. Use the service filters in the clinic list on this page to see which ones.

What is the best-ranked IVF clinic in South Dakota?

By the FertilityList Score, which weighs CDC-reported live-birth rates against national figures by age, cycle volume, reporting consistency, SART membership and lab accreditation, the highest-ranked clinic in South Dakota is Sanford Women's Health (3.0 out of 5).
