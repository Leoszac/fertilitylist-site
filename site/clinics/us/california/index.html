California IVF clinics: common questions

How many IVF clinics are in California?

85 clinics in California reported IVF outcomes to the CDC for 2022, performing 71,445 ART cycles in total. Another 12 clinics appear in CDC records without 2022 outcome data.

Which fertility clinic in California performs the most IVF cycles?
