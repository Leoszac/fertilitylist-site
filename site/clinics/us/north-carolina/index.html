North Carolina IVF clinics: common questions

How many IVF clinics are in North Carolina?

11 clinics in North Carolina reported IVF outcomes to the CDC for 2022, performing 9,004 ART cycles in total.

Which fertility clinic in North Carolina performs the most IVF cycles?

Carolinas Fertility Institute reported the most ART cycles in North Carolina for 2022: 2,307. Cycle volume reflects experience but is not by itself a measure of success.

What is the IVF success rate in North Carolina?

For patients under 35 using their own eggs, the median across 10 reporting clinics in North Carolina was 55.4% live births per intended egg retrieval in 2022, compared with 49.7% nationally (CDC). Rates fall with age and vary by clinic.

Which clinics in North Carolina offer egg freezing, donor eggs or gestational carriers?

According to the services clinics reported to the CDC for 2022, 11 of 11 offer egg freezing, 10 offer donor eggs and 10 work with gestational carriers. Use the service filters in the clinic list on this page to see which ones.

What is the best-ranked IVF clinic in North Carolina?

By the FertilityList Score, which weighs CDC-reported live-birth rates against national figures by age, cycle volume, reporting consistency, SART membership and lab accreditation, the highest-ranked clinic in North Carolina is Carolinas Fertility Institute (4.9 out of 5). The CDC cautions that comparing clinics may not be meaningful because clinics treat different patient populations.
