New Jersey IVF clinics: common questions

How many IVF clinics are in New Jersey?

14 clinics in New Jersey reported IVF outcomes to the CDC for 2022, performing 20,809 ART cycles in total. Another 3 clinics appear in CDC records without 2022 outcome data.

Which fertility clinic in New Jersey performs the most IVF cycles?

Reproductive Medicine Associates of New Jersey reported the most ART cycles in New Jersey for 2022: 11,232. Cycle volume reflects experience but is not by itself a measure of success.

What is the IVF success rate in New Jersey?

For patients under 35 using their own eggs, the median across 13 reporting clinics in New Jersey was 48.9% live births per intended egg retrieval in 2022, compared with 49.7% nationally (CDC). Rates fall with age and vary by clinic.

Which clinics in New Jersey offer egg freezing, donor eggs or gestational carriers?

According to the services clinics reported to the CDC for 2022, 14 of 14 offer egg freezing, 14 offer donor eggs and 14 work with gestational carriers. Use the service filters in the clinic list on this page to see which ones.

What is the best-ranked IVF clinic in New Jersey?

By the FertilityList Score, which weighs CDC-reported live-birth rates against national figures by age, cycle volume, reporting consistency, SART membership and lab accreditation, the highest-ranked clinic in New Jersey is Reproductive Medicine Associates of New Jersey (4.9 out of 5). The CDC cautions that comparing clinics may not be meaningful because clinics treat different patient populations.
