New York IVF clinics: common questions

How many IVF clinics are in New York, NY?

24 clinics in New York, NY reported IVF outcomes to the CDC for 2022, performing 39,229 ART cycles in total.

Which fertility clinic in New York, NY performs the most IVF cycles?

Weill Cornell Medicine, Center for Reproductive Medicine reported the most ART cycles in New York, NY for 2022: 6,395. Cycle volume reflects experience but is not by itself a measure of success.

What is the IVF success rate in New York, NY?

For patients under 35 using their own eggs, the median across 19 reporting clinics in New York, NY was 47.6% live births per intended egg retrieval in 2022, compared with 49.7% nationally (CDC). Rates fall with age and vary by clinic.

Which clinics in New York, NY offer egg freezing, donor eggs or gestational carriers?

According to the services clinics reported to the CDC for 2022, 24 of 24 offer egg freezing, 21 offer donor eggs and 21 work with gestational carriers. Use the service filters in the clinic list on this page to see which ones.

What is the best-ranked IVF clinic in New York, NY?

By the FertilityList Score, which weighs CDC-reported live-birth rates against national figures by age, cycle volume, reporting consistency, SART membership and lab accreditation, the highest-ranked clinic in New York, NY is Reproductive Medicine Associates of New York, LLP (4.8 out of 5). The CDC cautions that comparing clinics may not be meaningful because clinics treat different patient populations.
