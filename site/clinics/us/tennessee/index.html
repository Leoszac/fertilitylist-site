Tennessee IVF clinics: common questions

How many IVF clinics are in Tennessee?

7 clinics in Tennessee reported IVF outcomes to the CDC for 2022, performing 4,675 ART cycles in total. Another 1 clinic appear in CDC records without 2022 outcome data.

Which fertility clinic in Tennessee performs the most IVF cycles?

Nashville Fertility Center reported the most ART cycles in Tennessee for 2022: 1,590. Cycle volume reflects experience but is not by itself a measure of success.

What is the IVF success rate in Tennessee?

For patients under 35 using their own eggs, the median across 6 reporting clinics in Tennessee was 47.5% live births per intended egg retrieval in 2022, compared with 49.7% nationally (CDC). Rates fall with age and vary by clinic.

Which clinics in Tennessee offer egg freezing, donor eggs or gestational carriers?

According to the services clinics reported to the CDC for 2022, 7 of 7 offer egg freezing, 5 offer donor eggs and 5 work with gestational carriers. Use the service filters in the clinic list on this page to see which ones.

What is the best-ranked IVF clinic in Tennessee?

By the FertilityList Score, which weighs CDC-reported live-birth rates against national figures by age, cycle volume, reporting consistency, SART membership and lab accreditation, the highest-ranked clinic in Tennessee is Kutteh Ke Fertility Associates of Memphis, PLLC (4.6 out of 5). The CDC cautions that comparing clinics may not be meaningful because clinics treat different patient populations.
